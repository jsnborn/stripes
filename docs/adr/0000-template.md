# ADR NNNN: Short title in the form of a decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR NNNN](./NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **PR:** #N

## Context

The forces at play. What makes this a decision rather than an obvious choice?
State the constraint honestly, including anything that pushes the other way.

## Decision

What was chosen, in one or two sentences, in the active voice. "We will..."

## Alternatives considered

Each real alternative and why it lost. An ADR with no alternatives is a note,
not a decision record. If there genuinely was only one option, say so and say
why.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder. The second half is the part that
makes an ADR worth reading two years later, so do not leave it empty.
